Questions

Where do the mats go?

On the folding tables inside participating laundromats, at least 10 mats per location, with the owner's permission.

Why laundromats?

People spend real time there. While they fold, the mat sits right in front of them, so your offer gets more than a quick glance.

How do customers use my offer?

They scan your QR code, enter their name and mobile number, and get your coupon by text. They can also call your phone number on the mat.

What do I get back?

Counts of scans, claimed coupons and delivered coupons, plus redemptions when they can be verified. A scan is not a sale, and we report them separately.

Is there an AI receptionist?

No. This product is the mat, your QR coupon page and text delivery. Calls go straight to your own number.

When does my coupon expire?

Every coupon on the mat expires on the same fixed campaign end date, printed on your ad and shown on your coupon page.

Is $299 a monthly fee?

No. It is $299 total for one 60-day campaign.

I own a laundromat. What is in it for me?

The center of every mat promotes your loyalty program, app or offer at no cost to you. Host compensation is worked out with each owner.

Do you guarantee customers or sales?

No. We track responses honestly and share what can be verified.
