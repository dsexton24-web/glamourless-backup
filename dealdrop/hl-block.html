The Deal Drop standard

These rules are why homeowners keep the card on the fridge.

A real deal or no spot

A specific dollar amount, percentage, package price or paid upgrade. Free estimates and "call for details" do not qualify.

One business per category

No competitor shares your edition. Six different businesses on each side, twelve in total.

Clear, printed terms

Minimum spend, limits and exclusions are printed on the card in readable type, not hidden behind the QR code.

One fixed expiration date

Every offer expires 40 days after the print order is placed. About 10 days to print and mail, about 30 days for shoppers to claim.

You approve the proof

Nothing prints until you sign off on your ad exactly as it will appear.

Honest offers only

No made-up regular prices or inflated savings. We turn down offers that could mislead a shopper.

Questions

Who receives the card?

About 5,000 homes on USPS mail routes selected for estimated average household income of $100K to $200K, based on Census-level estimates. Every home on a selected route gets the card.

Why only one business per category?

So your offer is the only one of its kind on the card. Shoppers looking for a plumber see one plumber: you.

What counts as a real deal?

A specific, meaningful benefit: a dollar amount off, a percentage, a set package price, or a paid upgrade at no charge. Free estimates, "best prices" and "call for details" do not count.

What does the AI receptionist actually do?

It answers your extension as your business, explains your approved offer, answers common questions, and books appointments only into time slots you approve, with at least 48 hours of lead time. It never makes up availability. Urgent and emergency calls route to your own line.

I do not take appointments. Does this still work?

Yes. For businesses that do not book appointments, shoppers claim or reserve the offer instead, and you get their details right away.

Do I need a website?

No. Your QR code opens a deal page we build for you for this campaign.

When do I pay, and what if the edition does not print?

You pay at checkout, in full or half down with the rest charged 14 days later. If the edition does not go to print, every payment is refunded in full.

Who owns the leads?

You do. Every inquiry and booking for your business is sent to you, and nothing is held back.

Can I come back for the next edition?

Yes. Current advertisers get first chance to keep their category in the next edition.
